For DoubleBee paper edits (ICRA 2027), the user approved this style on 2026-09-15:

- Natural academic prose that does not read as AI. No flourishes ("exactly where
  that boundary sits", "sharply asymmetric in its economics", "Such behaviour"),
  no grand opener sentences ("Because the policy is deployed without further
  training, the simulator must...").
- Short, direct sentences, but joined with connecting words ("and", "so",
  "since", "neither driving nor flying but rather"). Varied openers. Never repeat
  the same opener pattern ("To improve..., To build..., To account...").
- No colons or semicolons in prose. No em dashes. British English. Say "step",
  never "riser".
- Keep every technical detail when rewriting; do not silently drop numbers.
- 2026-09-15 reviewer-style feedback on the whole draft: it sounds too AI, the
  presentation is not smooth, there are too many numbers and not enough basic
  explanation, and it is too succinct. So: explain the idea in plain words
  first, then give only the numbers that support it (move secondary numbers to
  tables/figures). Write connected paragraphs that lead into each other.
  No catchy words or punchy phrases ("buys", "confines", "pays for", "holds what
  it gains", "the arrival that criterion was written to require"). Plain,
  scientific register. Fuller sentences over compressed ones.
- Always show a diff of what changed, then the full text.
- Minimal changes when asked for one or two words; do not add more.
- Apply ONLY the change asked for. Suggest other fixes separately and ask first;
  never fold unrequested edits into returned tex (2026-09-15: user asked for
  booktabs only and was annoyed a group label was renamed too).
- Do not repeat abstract content in figure captions or the title in the abstract.

**Why:** the user rejected several drafts as "reads so fucking ai" and "to to to",
and approved the version with varied openers and a plain closing clause.
**How to apply:** any paper paragraph rewrite in this project.
