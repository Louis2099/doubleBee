In the DoubleBee paper always write **step** or **steps climbed**. Never
"riser". Applies to body text, figure captions, axis labels and table headers.

**Why:** asked for twice, on 2026-09-08 the second time with visible irritation
after I reintroduced "riser" in drafted LaTeX having already switched the figure
to "steps climbed". It is a consistency issue across the paper, not a
preference to be re-litigated.

**How to apply:** grep any drafted section for "riser" before handing it over.
Places it has already crept in: the energy subsection ("episodes reaching one
riser"), the energy figure caption ("pinned to 6 cm risers"), the actuator
identification section ("rotate a wheel over a 6 cm riser"), and Section II-A of
the system overview. Related standing preferences: no em dashes, no underlining,
deliver LaTeX inline rather than writing it to files. See
[[doublebee-paper-status]].
